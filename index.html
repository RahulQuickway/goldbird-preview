FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Sector-51, Noida and serve Noida and nearby NCR, including Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Delhi NCR. Call us to confirm coverage for your site.

How quickly can you deploy security guards or staff?

After we understand your requirement and visit the site, we plan deployment as quickly as possible. The timeline depends on the number of people, shifts and roles — call or WhatsApp us and we will give you a clear date.

Are your guards and staff background-verified?

Yes. Guards and staff go through ID and address verification before they are deployed at a client site.

Can I hire pantry boys or office boys only?

Yes. You can hire a single pantry boy or office boy, or combine them with housekeeping, front-desk and security staff.

What if a guard or staff member is absent?

We arrange a replacement as quickly as possible so your post is covered, and a supervisor keeps you informed.

Do you offer short-term or event staff?

Yes. Along with monthly contracts, we provide bouncers and event security, and short-term or seasonal staff.
