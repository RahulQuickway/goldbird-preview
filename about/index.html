Who we are

One partner for security and facility staff

GOLDBIRD SECURITY & FACILITY SERVICES PVT. LTD. is a one-stop partner for security and facility management services. From your gate to your pantry, we provide trained guards, office boys, housekeeping teams, front-desk staff, cleaners and drivers — and we stay accountable for them after deployment.

Our clients range from households and offices to residential societies, industrial units and event organisers. We believe in long-term relationships, so every service is planned around the individual client.
